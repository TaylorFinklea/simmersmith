SimmerSmith Privacy Policy

SimmerSmith uses your iCloud account for synchronization and household sharing. It does not operate a central account system or meal-planning database. Optional AI features send requests directly from your device to a provider you configure.

1. Information SimmerSmith stores

Shared household data in iCloud may include:

  • Recipes, ingredients, cooking steps, recipe images, and recipe memories
  • Weekly meal plans, grocery lists, pantry items, and household settings
  • Events, guest names, age groups, allergies, and dietary notes you enter
  • Custom household terms and other content created by household members

This data is stored in a private CloudKit zone associated with the household owner's iCloud account. If the owner shares the household, Apple makes that zone available to the invited participants who accept the share.

Personal data in iCloud may include:

  • Your dietary goals, ingredient preferences, avoidances, and allergy flags
  • Your AI assistant conversations and personal assistant settings

Personal data uses your private iCloud database and is not included in the household share.

Data stored on your device may include:

  • Supported AI-provider API keys in the iOS Keychain
  • Local app preferences and the Apple Reminders list you select
  • A local cache used for responsive and offline operation
  • Rolling household backup files in the app's Application Support directory

2. Household sharing

A participant who accepts an iCloud household share can access the household data listed above, including guest information and dietary or allergy notes saved to the household. Sharing is household-wide rather than record-by-record. Your personal iCloud data and your API keys are not shared with household participants.

Owners can stop sharing and participants can leave using the app and Apple's iCloud sharing controls. Removing access does not erase information a participant already saw, exported, or retained while they had access.

3. Optional AI features

SimmerSmith can process some AI tasks on your device. For cloud AI features, you choose a supported provider and supply your own API key. Current choices include OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama Cloud, and NeuralWatt for text features, and OpenAI or Google Gemini for recipe images. The available choices may change as the app evolves.

Requests go directly from your device to the selected provider. SimmerSmith does not proxy those requests through a developer-operated server. Depending on the feature and your request, transmitted information can include:

  • Recipes, meal plans, groceries, pantry items, and household terminology
  • Your dietary goals, preferences, avoidances, and allergy flags
  • Event and guest details, including names, allergies, and dietary notes
  • Text you type or dictate, assistant conversation context, or image prompts
  • A photo you explicitly choose for an AI-powered image-analysis feature

The selected provider's privacy policy and terms govern its handling of a request after it receives the request. You can avoid third-party AI transmission by not configuring a provider key. Non-AI planning, recipe, grocery, and sharing features remain available.

4. API keys

AI-provider keys are stored as device-only iOS Keychain items. They are not written to CloudKit, included in SimmerSmith household backups, or synchronized through iCloud Keychain by the app. Clearing a key in Settings deletes that provider's saved Keychain item.

5. Photos, camera, microphone, and speech

  • Photos: Apple's system photo picker lets you select specific images for recipe import, recipe photos, memories, and visual checks. SimmerSmith receives the items you select, not general access to your photo library.
  • Camera: used when you choose a camera-based recipe scan.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used when you enable hands-free cooking commands. Recognition is performed on-device when supported; Apple's speech service may be used when on-device recognition is unavailable. Voice week planning uses the system keyboard's dictation or text entry. A resulting text transcript may be sent to your selected AI provider when cloud parsing is needed.

6. Apple Reminders and notifications

If you enable grocery synchronization, SimmerSmith requests full Reminders access so it can create items in, and reconcile changes from, the single Reminders list you choose. It does not intentionally read or modify your other lists. Grocery names, quantities, units, store labels, notes, and completion state may be exchanged with that list.

If you grant notification permission, SimmerSmith can schedule local meal and grocery reminders on your device. The app also uses silent Apple push notifications for CloudKit synchronization. It does not send visible notification content from a SimmerSmith server.

7. Backups, retention, and deletion

SimmerSmith can create rolling JSON snapshots of household records in the app's local Application Support directory. You can delete those files in the app. Their inclusion in device backups is controlled by Apple's operating system and your device settings.

Household and personal records remain in iCloud until they are deleted through the app or Apple's applicable iCloud controls. Deleting the app removes its local data but may not delete data already stored in iCloud. Because the developer does not operate the data store, support cannot directly inspect or delete your iCloud records for you.

8. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. SimmerSmith does not sell personal information or use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when SimmerSmith's features or data practices change. The date below identifies the current version.
