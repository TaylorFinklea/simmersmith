SimmerSmith

Plan meals, keep recipes and grocery lists together, and share a household through iCloud. Optional AI features use an API provider and key you configure on your device.

Get Support Read Privacy Policy

Plan together

Keep recipes, weekly meal plans, groceries, pantry items, and events in one household that can be shared with people you invite through iCloud.

Your data plane

Household and personal data sync through your iCloud account. SimmerSmith does not operate a central meal-planning database or account service.

Optional AI

Add your own supported provider key to use AI planning and image features. Keys stay in this device's Keychain and requests go directly to the provider you select.
